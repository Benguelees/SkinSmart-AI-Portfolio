SkinSmart AI is a personalized skincare recommendation system.

The SkinSmart dataset contains skincare product information that can be used to compare products and match them to user preferences.

For each product, the system can store information such as:
- product ID
- brand
- product name
- category
- ingredients
- simplified ingredients
- whether the product contains fragrance
- whether it contains drying alcohol
- whether it contains parabens
- whether it contains sulfates
- whether it contains silicones

SkinSmart uses product characteristics and ingredient-related information to recommend products that better match a user's skincare preferences and needs.

The recommendation system can exclude products that conflict with a user's hard ingredient preferences.

SkinSmart can also explain why a product was recommended by referring to the product's characteristics and ingredient-related information.

SkinSmart is designed as a skincare recommendation system. It is not intended to provide medical diagnoses or clinical treatment advice.
